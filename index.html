NEOGUARD

A Multilayer Smart Textile System for Neonatal Thermal Care and Vital Monitoring

NeoGuard is a portable smart-textile concept designed to support newborn thermal care while monitoring important vital parameters. It combines flexible conductive heating fabric, temperature sensing, heart-rate and SpO₂ monitoring, and automatic safety control in a multilayer textile structure.

The system is intended to support neonatal care in rural and hilly areas, small clinics, primary healthcare centres, neonatal transport, and home or community-care settings.

Overview

About NeoGuard

A dedicated exploration in combining flexible thermal actuation with non-invasive vital sensing in a practical, textile-native form factor.

NeoGuard is designed to combine thermal support and basic vital monitoring in a portable textile-based system.

The blanket uses a three-layer structure. Soft textile material forms the outer layers, while the middle layer incorporates flexible conductive heating fabric. Sensors collect temperature and vital-sign readings, and an ESP32-WROOM controller processes the data.

Based on predefined temperature limits, the controller manages the heating system and monitors safety conditions. Sensor readings can also be transmitted for remote viewing and stored for later reference.

The aim is to develop a compact and accessible monitoring platform for settings where advanced neonatal monitoring facilities may be limited.

Thermal Support

Temperature-based control of flexible conductive heating fabric to supply calibrated warming within predefined boundaries.

Vital Monitoring

Heart-rate and SpO₂ monitoring alongside temperature measurement to keep continuous track of physiological stability.

Connected Monitoring

Sensor-data display, alerts, and historical records through a connected interface for timely clinical insight.

Medical Boundary Notice: NeoGuard is designed to combine thermal support and basic vital monitoring in a portable textile-based system. NeoGuard does not replace an incubator, neonatal intensive care unit (NICU), or qualified medical professionals.

Context

The Challenge

Understanding the clinical and logistical realities faced during neonatal thermal management in low-resource environments.

01

Heat Loss Vulnerability

Preterm and low-birth-weight newborns are more vulnerable to rapid heat loss due to high body-surface-area-to-weight ratios and limited subcutaneous tissue.

02

Continuous Thermal Balance

Maintaining a suitable thermal environment requires careful and continuous monitoring to avert both hypothermic drop and dangerous hyperthermia.

03

Remote Setting Limitations

Continuous monitoring facilities may be limited in some rural and remote settings where specialized instrumentation is unavailable.

04

Equipment Inaccessibility

Conventional warming and monitoring equipment may not be easily accessible or transportable in every care environment, transit vehicle, or community clinic.

05

Caregiver Clarity Needs

Caregivers need clear, real-time information about temperature, vital readings, and abnormal operating conditions to take informed, timely actions.

System Architecture

How It Works

A closed-loop pipeline executing continuous sensing, autonomous threshold comparison, controlled thermal actuation, and multi-channel telemetry.

STEP 1

SENSE

Sensors collect temperature and vital-sign readings directly from the infant and environment.

STEP 2

PROCESS

The ESP32-WROOM receives and processes sensor signals through digital filtering.

STEP 3

COMPARE

The measured temperature is compared with predefined safe operating limits (36.5–37.5°C).

STEP 4

RESPOND

The controller activates heating when needed and triggers automatic cut-off if limits are exceeded.

STEP 5

MONITOR

Readings can be displayed locally on OLED/Serial or transmitted via Wi-Fi for remote viewing.

STEP 6

ALERT

The interface informs caregivers when monitored readings or system conditions require attention.

Sense → Process → Compare → Respond → Monitor

* The system operates closed-loop feedback control for thermal regulation. It does not independently diagnose medical conditions.

Ambient Side

Top Layer — Soft Textile

  • Soft textile material designed for gentle contact with sensitive newborn skin.
  • Intended to provide a comfortable outer surface while maintaining vapor permeability.
  • Buffers infant skin from direct contact with internal electronic components.

Middle Layer — Heating and Sensor Integration

  • Flexible conductive heating fabric functioning as a distributed low-voltage warming element.
  • Provision for embedded sensor placement (DS18B20 temperature probe and MAX30102 optical sensor).
  • Designed to support controlled thermal operation with uniform heat dissipation.

Bottom Layer — Soft Textile

  • Soft textile material forming the lower layer of the proposed blanket structure.
  • Minimizes downward thermal leakage toward external surfaces.
  • Protects internal wiring, busbars, and sensor leads from external mechanical snagging.
Hardware & Software

Technology Behind NeoGuard

Engineered around standard, robust embedded components and cloud infrastructure for verifiable, reproducible prototyping.

Controller

ESP32-WROOM

Main controller for sensor acquisition, real-time signal processing, heating relay actuation, and Wi-Fi communication.

Temperature

DS18B20

High-precision digital temperature measurement on a 1-Wire bus for continuous, calibrated infant body temperature monitoring.

Vitals Sensing

MAX30102

Integrated pulse oximetry and heart-rate sensor utilizing dual red/infrared optical LEDs and photodetector via I2C interface.

Environment

DHT11

Ambient temperature and humidity monitoring to evaluate microclimate stability surrounding the neonatal blanket.

Actuation

Conductive Fabric

Flexible conductive heating fabric operating as a low-voltage heating element embedded in the middle textile layer.

Protection

Relay & Cut-Off

Dedicated isolation relays that immediately disconnect heating power upon detection of predefined unsafe thermal conditions.

Cloud Database

Firebase

Cloud data storage and retrieval via Realtime Database for synchronizing telemetry frames, alerts, and access-controlled accounts.

Mobile Platform

Android Application

Dedicated interface for viewing live readings, alert notifications, and historical trends directly on healthcare workers' phones.

Direct Interface

Serial/USB Monitoring

Direct Web Serial API connection to a local computer for offline monitoring, benchtop diagnostics, and edge debugging.

Sensor Acquisition: Raw readings are acquired by the ESP32 microcontroller at deterministic intervals.

Wi-Fi Transmission: Collected telemetry packages can be transmitted over local Wi-Fi directly to Firebase cloud endpoints.

Mobile Retrieval: The Android application retrieves and visualizes active readings for bedside or remote checking.

Historical Audit: Past telemetry records can be rendered as trend curves or exported as CSV for subsequent review.

Local PC Support: When internet connectivity is absent, a local computer can monitor readings directly through the serial/USB interface without data loss.

Safety Engineering

Safety Comes First

Prioritizing infant well-being through layered fail-safe mechanisms, deterministic heating boundaries, and fault containment.

Temperature Monitoring

Continuously evaluates available temperature readings with digital resolution down to 0.0625°C to catch thermal drift early.

Controlled Heating

Activates heating strictly according to the configured safe temperature range (36.5°C to 37.5°C) to prevent thermal instability.

Automatic Cut-Off

Immediately disconnects the heating relay when configured unsafe conditions or temperatures exceeding upper limits are detected.

Sensor-Fault Handling

Handles missing, disconnected, or invalid sensor readings according to safe fallback logic, de-energizing the heater until sensor health is verified.

Prototype Notice: NeoGuard is a developing prototype. Its thermal performance, sensor accuracy, electrical safety, textile safety, and clinical suitability require appropriate testing and validation before use with newborns.
